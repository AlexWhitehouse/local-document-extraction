# Plans And Limits

Workspace plans control API access, Template capacity, member capacity, monthly page limits, and Credits.

For how these plans behave inside a Workspace and why a customer would choose each one, see [Plans](../usage/plans.md).

## Self-Service Plans

| Plan | Monthly Price | Included Credits | API Access | Templates | Top-Level Fields | Table Fields | Columns Per Table | Members | Monthly Pages |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | GBP 0 | 0 | No | 3 | 5 | 1 | 5 | 3 | 500 |
| Pro | GBP 50 | 200 | Yes | 10 | 15 | 1 | 10 | 50 | 1500 |
| Max | GBP 200 | 1000 | Yes | 50 | 20 | 1 | 15 | Unlimited | 5000 |

Prices are catalog prices before tax unless otherwise stated.

## Per-Page Catalog Prices

| Plan | Credit Pack Price Per Page |
| --- | ---: |
| Free | GBP 0.22 |
| Pro | GBP 0.20 |
| Max | GBP 0.18 |

Credit packs are available in 100, 500, 1000, and 5000 Credit sizes when the active entitlement is Free, Pro, or Max.

## Enforcement Points

Limits are enforced when:

- Creating or updating Templates.
- Inviting Workspace members.
- Rotating Workspace API keys.
- Submitting Documents.
- Reserving Credits and page capacity for accepted Document submissions.

Frontend disabled states are advisory. Backend enforcement is authoritative.
