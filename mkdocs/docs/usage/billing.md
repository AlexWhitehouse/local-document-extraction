# Billing

Workspace billing is owned by the Workspace owner. The active entitlement controls Credits, monthly page capacity, member limits, Template limits, and API access.

For a plan-by-plan explanation of Workspace behavior and benefits, see [Plans](plans.md).

## Credits

A Credit allows one billable Document page to be submitted for extraction.

- PDF Documents are billed by detected page count.
- Image Documents are billed as one page.
- Paid plans include monthly Credits.
- Credit packs add Purchased Credits that can be used after included Credits.
- Failed queue acceptance paths refund reserved Credits when the submission is not accepted.

## Owner Billing Section

Workspace owners can view:

- Active entitlement.
- Available Credits.
- Current billing period usage.
- Remaining current-period page capacity.
- Billing activity.
- Credit usage charts.
- Subscription and Checkout actions.

Non-owner members do not see owner billing details. They may see limited billing operational status when billing blocks product actions.

## Self-Service Plans

Self-service plans are Free, Pro, and Max. Pro and Max include API access and paid subscription management.

Owners can:

- Start subscription Checkout for Pro or Max.
- Upgrade from Pro to Max.
- Manage subscription changes from the billing section.
- Buy Credit packs of 100, 500, 1000, or 5000 Credits.

## Custom And Enterprise Plans

Some Workspaces may have custom or Enterprise terms arranged outside the self-service subscription flow. When this applies, the app shows the active entitlement and relevant usage or invoice information available to the Workspace owner.
