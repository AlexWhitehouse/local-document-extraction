# Document and packet model costs

Track endpoint-reported model costs independently of the configured provider. Normalize documented response conventions at the shared Model gateway boundary, in this order: `usage.cost`, `cost_breakdown.total_cost`, then `x-litellm-response-cost`. These conventions use USD. Select one finite nonnegative total; preserve zero and never add overlapping components. An explicitly different currency is unavailable for USD accounting. An endpoint that omits cost still processes normally. No model price list or token-based estimate is applied.

Minimal Workspace-owned accounting records extend the latest-attempt-only metadata rule from ADR-0008 and the Backend Context. Record a unique local call identity, owner, packet lineage, stage, model/configuration revision, timestamps, request identifier, token counts, reported amount and its source. Do not retain request or response content, arbitrary response metadata, or credentials. This exception is required to include billable retries and reassessments without duplicate accounting.

Persist a pending receipt immediately before transport and finalize it once at the gateway boundary, before application response validation. Failed validation can incur cost. Interrupted calls and responses without usable cost remain unknown. A failed receipt finalization leaves a pending unknown receipt and must not repeat a billable request. Historical attempts migrate as unknown rather than invented zeroes. These figures are reported call costs, not invoice reconciliation; unobserved gateway-internal charges cannot be reconstructed by the application.

Use `split`, `auto_template`, and `extraction` stages. Each Document owns its direct selection/extraction calls. A packet owns its split calls and rolls up its children's direct calls once. Allocate split cost as `packet split cost * document pages / selected packet pages`, preserving precision until display. Unselected pages do not enter the denominator. Excluded pages keep their share at packet level; an all-blank packet keeps the full split cost. No-cost stages have zero incurred cost; unavailable stages are not zero. A total with unknown calls is marked incomplete and carries the known subtotal, or null when none of its calls reported cost.

Child deletion preserves incurred costs in the parent packet and does not redistribute split allocations. Packet deletion removes parent and child receipts. Standalone Document deletion removes its receipts. Workspace erasure removes all accounting data. Cost snapshots are included in job HTTP validators, live-update summaries, and the completed-document cache.

Document and packet headers show the total with an accessible hover/focus/tap breakdown for the three stages. Document tooltips explain page allocation; packet tooltips explain excluded-page overhead. In-progress work shows costs so far, and unavailable or partial costs remain distinguishable from zero.
