# v2.5.1 — Command palette conditions

This release lets command palette searches combine conditions with "and" and "or" ([#96](https://github.com/AlexWhitehouse/local-document-extraction/pull/96)). It also fixes several ways a request could be read more broadly than it was written ([#97](https://github.com/AlexWhitehouse/local-document-extraction/pull/97)).

## Command palette

- **Several conditions:** every condition in a request is now used. Before, only the first applied. For example, in "prescription documents that cost more than $0.03 and less than $0.05" the second bound was dropped, or applied to an unrelated number field.
- **"Or" between conditions:** conditions can be alternatives, on one field or across fields and templates. For example:
  - "vendor is Acme, Globex or Initech"
  - "invoice number is X1 or X2", or "total is 5 or 10 or 15"
  - "total under 10 or over 100", or "cost under 1 cent or over 5 cents"
  - "issued before 1 March or after 1 June"
  - "invoices with total over 100 or receipts with total over 50"
- **How a request is grouped:**
  - Conditions on one field come together first, so "vendor is Acme or Globex and total over 100" means (Acme or Globex) and over 100.
  - Otherwise "and" comes before "or", so "vendor is Acme or total over 500 and paid" means Acme, or (over 500 and paid).
  - In a list such as "A, B or C", the commas take the word before the last item.
  - Two exact values of one field joined by "and" mean either one, as in "invoices from Acme and Globex". "Excluding Acme or Globex" leaves out both.
- **One-letter values:** "starts with A", "is A" and "contains a" are now read as conditions. Before, "the patient name is A" was read as "patient name has a value" and matched every document.
- **Summaries:** a field is named once per group, for example "Invoice › Total under 10 or over 100". Brackets show how groups nest.
- **"Not used" notes:** when the assistant model reads a request, the note lists only the words it couldn't use. Before, it also listed words the model had used.

## Fixes

- **Combining the palette's reading with the model's:** a cost the palette recognised could be dropped when the model corrected a field in the same group. A request such as "delete invoices that cost over $0.03 or total under 5 billed to Leeds" could then act on every invoice. A condition the palette read is now kept unless its replacement keeps the same cost and is used.
- **Unfinished comparisons:** a field followed by a comparison the palette couldn't read, such as "invoice number is" with nothing after it, became "has a value". Now the palette leaves it for the model. Closing brackets and quotes also end a value, so "(where invoice number is A)" searches for "A".
- **False "no document can match":** this note appeared when one "or" alternative needed two templates at once, even though another alternative could match. It now appears only when no alternative can match.

## API

- **Grouped conditions:** in `POST /v1/jobs/search`, and in filters returned by `POST /v1/search/interpret`, each member of `filter.conditions` is one of:
  - a field condition, as before
  - a cost condition, `{ "cost": { "op", "value", "value2"? } }`
  - a group: `{ "any": [...] }` holds when at least one member does, and `{ "all": [...] }` when every member does.
- **Limits:** groups nest at most 3 deep, and the list holds at most 10 field and cost conditions in total, counted at every depth. Lists that break these limits return `400 invalid_search_filter`.
- **Cost:** filters returned by the API no longer have a separate `cost` member. Cost conditions sit in `conditions`. Requests may still send `cost` as one condition or a list of them; it's read as cost conditions added to `conditions`.
- **Cost search:** filtering by cost anywhere in the tree still returns `403 forbidden` for callers who can't read costs.

See the [API overview](https://github.com/AlexWhitehouse/local-document-extraction/blob/v2.5.1/mkdocs/docs/api/overview.md).

## Upgrading and compatibility

The release workflow checks **Ubuntu 24.04 x64 and arm64, and macOS 15 arm64**. Intel macOS binaries are bundled but not qualified by the CI matrix.

Stop the application. Back up its configuration and state. Then run:

```bash
document-extraction update v2.5.1
```

- **Databases:** no migrations.
- **Settings:** no settings or defaults change.
- **API clients:** a client that reads `filter.cost` from a search interpretation should read cost conditions from `filter.conditions` instead. Requests in the v2.5.0 form keep working.

## Known limits

- Statuses, upload dates and templates can't be grouped with field or cost conditions. A request such as "failed invoices or vendor is Acme" goes to the model, which reports the part it can't use.
- Mixes of "and" and "or" that would nest groups more than 3 deep are passed to the model rather than guessed.
- Search covers scalar fields only: text, number, date and yes/no.
- Plain-language requests that need the model only work when a Model gateway is configured.
