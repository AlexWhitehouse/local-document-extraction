# Enterprise Annual Commitment And Overage

Category: enhancement
Type: AFK
Status: completed

## Parent

.scratch/workspace-billing/PRD.md

## What to build

Add Enterprise annual commitments and monthly overage billing. Application admins should be able to create annual deal terms using standard defaults or custom monthly allowance/per-page price. The yearly cost is derived automatically. Enterprise annual entitlement activates only after the upfront invoice is paid. Monthly overage invoices are generated from Billing ledger usage above the monthly minimum allowance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Application admins can create Enterprise annual commitments with monthly minimum allowance, per-page price, billing cycle start date, collection method, and reason.
- [ ] Standard defaults are available: 60,000/month at £0.09 and £64,800/year; 100,000/month at £0.08 and £96,000/year; 200,000/month at £0.07 and £168,000/year.
- [ ] Application admins can enter custom monthly allowance and per-page price.
- [ ] Yearly cost is derived automatically from monthly allowance times 12 times per-page price.
- [ ] Annual commitment upfront invoice is visible and payable by the Workspace owner.
- [ ] Annual entitlement activates only after the upfront invoice is paid.
- [ ] Application admins cannot manually activate unpaid Enterprise annual commitments; no-cost access uses No-billing mode.
- [ ] Enterprise annual usage up to the monthly minimum allowance has no additional monthly charge.
- [ ] Unused monthly minimum allowance does not roll over.
- [ ] Enterprise overage above the monthly minimum allowance is invoiced monthly in arrears at the commitment per-page price.
- [ ] Overage invoices are generated automatically after each monthly invoice period closes from Billing ledger usage.
- [ ] Overage invoices finalize automatically unless Enterprise invoice review mode is enabled.
- [ ] Overdue overage invoices suspend Enterprise entitlement until paid.
- [ ] Enterprise annual commitments can start after ramp-up or without ramp-up.
- [ ] Active Enterprise entitlement supersedes self-service subscription entitlement without double billing.
- [ ] Tests cover default/custom terms, derived yearly cost, upfront invoice activation, allowance/no-rollover behavior, overage calculation, invoice generation, overdue suspension, and fallback.
- [ ] `npm run typecheck --prefix backend` and `npm run build --prefix frontend` pass.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Add Enterprise annual commitments with upfront annual invoicing, monthly allowance, and monthly overage billing.

**Current behavior:**
Workspace billing supports the code-owned Free, Pro, and Max plans, prepaid Credits, self-service subscriptions, Application admin Plan overrides, payment-required override invoices, No-billing mode, and Enterprise ramp-up behavior. Enterprise ramp-up can use the Enterprise limit profile, record Enterprise usage charges, generate monthly usage invoices, reflect invoice state to owners and Application admins, and suspend or restore entitlement based on invoice payment state.

There is not yet an Enterprise annual commitment model. Application admins cannot assign annual deal terms with a monthly minimum allowance and overage per-page price, owners cannot pay an upfront annual commitment invoice, and Workspace billing cannot activate annual Enterprise entitlement only after that upfront payment. Overage above a monthly minimum allowance is not yet calculated or invoiced for annual commitments.

**Desired behavior:**
Application admins should be able to assign Enterprise annual commitment terms to a Workspace. Terms must include monthly minimum allowance, per-page price, billing cycle start date, collection method, optional invoice review mode, and a required reason. The UI/API should offer standard defaults for 60,000 pages/month at GBP 0.09, 100,000 pages/month at GBP 0.08, and 200,000 pages/month at GBP 0.07, while also allowing custom monthly allowance and per-page price. Yearly cost must be derived from monthly allowance times 12 times per-page price, not typed independently.

Creating annual commitment terms should create an upfront owner-visible Stripe invoice for the derived yearly cost. Annual Enterprise entitlement must activate only after that upfront invoice is paid. Application admins must not be able to manually activate unpaid annual commitments; no-cost access remains No-billing mode.

When an Enterprise annual commitment is active, accepted Document submissions should record Enterprise usage in the Billing ledger rather than spending prepaid Credits. Usage within the current monthly minimum allowance should create no additional monthly charge. Usage above the monthly minimum allowance should be invoiced monthly in arrears at the commitment per-page price. Unused monthly allowance must not roll over between invoice periods.

Annual commitments should be able to start directly or after Enterprise ramp-up. Active Enterprise entitlement should supersede active self-service subscription entitlement without spending Credits or double-billing. Overdue annual overage invoices should suspend Enterprise entitlement until paid. Suspended or expired annual entitlement should fall back to active self-service subscription entitlement if one exists, otherwise Free.

**Key interfaces:**
- Active entitlement resolver - should support active, pending-payment, suspended, and expired Enterprise annual commitment states with the same precedence rules as the PRD: No-billing, active Plan override, active Enterprise entitlement, active self-service subscription, then Free.
- Workspace billing control data - should store annual commitment deal terms, derived yearly amount, billing cycle start date, collection mode, invoice review mode, upfront invoice state, latest overage invoice state, suspension/expiry state, Application admin actor, reason, timestamps, and audit-relevant before/after snapshots.
- Billing period logic - should compute annual commitment monthly invoice periods from the Enterprise billing cycle start date, determine when an overage invoice period has closed, and avoid rolling unused allowance into later periods.
- Billing ledger RPC surface - should preserve Enterprise usage entries with Document submission metadata and support overage invoice summaries that separate allowance-covered pages from billable overage pages.
- Document submission billing authority - should record Enterprise annual usage before Source file storage and Extraction job creation when annual Enterprise entitlement is active.
- Stripe billing gateway - should create explicit invoice items and invoices for upfront annual charges and monthly overage charges, support automatic or manual collection, expose hosted invoice payment URLs when needed, and use stable idempotency keys for Stripe writes.
- Stripe billing webhook processing - should activate annual Enterprise entitlement after upfront invoice payment, update overage invoice state, suspend entitlement on overdue/failed overage invoices, restore entitlement after payment when the term is still active, and process duplicate events idempotently.
- Application admin billing API/UI - should let Application admins assign annual commitment terms, choose standard defaults or custom terms, inspect Enterprise status and invoice status, and see audit history.
- Owner Billing page contract - should show annual Enterprise entitlement, upfront invoice status, overage invoice status, hosted payment actions when applicable, and customer-facing Owner billing activity without exposing raw Stripe IDs or admin-only notes.
