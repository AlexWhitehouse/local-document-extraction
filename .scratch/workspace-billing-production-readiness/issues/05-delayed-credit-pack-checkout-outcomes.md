# Delayed Credit Pack Checkout Outcomes

Category: enhancement
Status: completed

## What to build

Support delayed-payment Checkout outcomes for Credit pack purchases. A delayed success should grant Purchased Credits exactly once through the same ledger authority as immediate paid Checkout, while a delayed failure should not grant Credits and should be visible enough for owner/admin follow-up.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded` for a Credit pack grants Purchased Credits exactly once using the existing idempotent ledger path.
- [x] `checkout.session.async_payment_failed` for a Credit pack does not grant Purchased Credits and records a visible failed payment state or activity.
- [x] Immediate `checkout.session.completed` with unpaid/processing delayed payment does not prematurely grant Credits.
- [x] Owner billing activity shows successful and failed delayed Credit pack outcomes where appropriate.
- [x] Application admin billing review can diagnose delayed payment failures without raw Stripe payloads.
- [x] Billing reconciliation detects missed delayed Credit pack success/failure outcomes and repairs only clearly paid missing grants.
- [x] Backend and frontend tests cover success, failure, duplicate delivery, missed-event reconciliation, and owner-visible failed delayed payment messaging.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Support delayed-payment Stripe Checkout outcomes for Credit pack purchases without granting unpaid Credits.

**Current behavior:**
Workspace billing can start Credit pack Checkout and grant Purchased Credits from an immediately paid Checkout completion or a paid Credit pack invoice. The Credit pack Checkout metadata includes the Workspace, billing action, plan, pack size, and purchase identifier needed for idempotent fulfillment. Immediate Checkout completion that is not paid does not grant Credits.

The Stripe billing webhook outcome store and explicit routing contract are already in place, and Application admin billing review can show ignored and failed Stripe billing event diagnostics. Owner billing activity is derived from the Billing ledger, so successful Purchased Credit grants are visible, but delayed-payment failure outcomes are not yet represented as owner-visible failed billing activity or admin-diagnosable Credit pack payment state.

**Desired behavior:**
When Stripe sends `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded` for a Credit pack purchase, Workspace billing should grant the purchased Credit pack exactly once through the same idempotent Billing ledger authority used by immediate paid Checkout and paid Credit pack invoices.

When Stripe sends `checkout.session.async_payment_failed` for a Credit pack purchase, Workspace billing should not grant Purchased Credits. The failure should be recorded as a visible failed payment state or billing activity for the Workspace owner where appropriate, and Application admin billing review should expose enough non-secret diagnostic detail to understand the failed delayed payment without storing raw Stripe payloads or payment method details.

Immediate `checkout.session.completed` events for delayed payment methods should remain non-granting while the payment is unpaid, processing, or otherwise not clearly paid. Billing reconciliation should detect missed delayed Credit pack success and failure outcomes. It may repair only clearly paid missing grants through the idempotent grant path; ambiguous or failed outcomes should become visible drift or diagnostics rather than granting Credits.

**Key interfaces:**
- Stripe billing webhook event router — should explicitly support delayed Checkout success and failure event types for Workspace billing.
- Credit pack Checkout fulfillment contract — should continue to rely on Checkout metadata for Workspace ID, billing action, Credit pack size, plan, and purchase ID.
- Billing ledger Purchased Credit grant operation — should remain the authority for granting Purchased Credits and should use the existing purchase-based idempotency behavior to suppress duplicate delivery.
- Owner billing activity shape — should show delayed Credit pack successes and failed delayed payment outcomes where those outcomes require owner follow-up.
- Application admin billing review state — should expose delayed Credit pack payment failures and missed-event diagnostics without raw Stripe payloads, secrets, payment method details, or unnecessary PII.
- Billing reconciliation — should compare Stripe-observed Credit pack Checkout and invoice state with Workspace billing records and repair only clearly paid missing grants.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded` for a Credit pack grants Purchased Credits exactly once through the idempotent Billing ledger path.
- [x] Duplicate delayed success delivery does not duplicate Purchased Credits, Owner billing activity, or admin diagnostics.
- [x] `checkout.session.async_payment_failed` for a Credit pack does not grant Purchased Credits.
- [x] Failed delayed Credit pack payment is visible to the Workspace owner as a failed payment state or activity where follow-up is appropriate.
- [x] Failed delayed Credit pack payment is visible to Application admins with non-secret diagnostic context and no raw Stripe payloads.
- [x] Immediate `checkout.session.completed` with unpaid, processing, or otherwise not clearly paid delayed payment state does not grant Credits.
- [x] Billing reconciliation detects missed delayed Credit pack success and failure outcomes, repairs only clearly paid missing grants, and records reviewable drift or diagnostics for failure/ambiguous cases.
- [x] Backend and frontend coverage exercises delayed success, delayed failure, duplicate delivery, missed-event reconciliation, and owner-visible failed delayed payment messaging.

**Out of scope:**
- Enabling or configuring delayed payment methods in Stripe Dashboard.
- Changing Credit pack sizes, prices, plan eligibility, currencies, tax behavior, or Workspace billing authority.
- Changing the Billing ledger authority model or rewriting historical ledger entries.
- Adding refunds, disputes, chargebacks, coupons, trials, or Stripe Customer Portal behavior.
- Storing raw Stripe webhook payload archives, API keys, webhook secrets, payment method details, or production secrets.
