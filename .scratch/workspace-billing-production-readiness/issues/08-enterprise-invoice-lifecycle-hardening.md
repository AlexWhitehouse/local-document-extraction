# Enterprise Invoice Lifecycle Hardening

Category: enhancement
Status: completed

## What to build

Harden Enterprise annual upfront, Enterprise annual overage, and Enterprise ramp-up invoice lifecycle handling. Paid invoices should activate or restore Enterprise entitlement where appropriate, while failed, action-required, finalization-failed, voided, and uncollectible states should suspend or keep payment-gated entitlement according to the existing Enterprise billing rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Enterprise annual upfront invoice customer-action, finalization-failed, payment-failed, voided, and uncollectible states do not activate Enterprise annual entitlement.
- [x] Enterprise annual upfront paid invoices activate the annual commitment exactly once and preserve hosted invoice state.
- [x] Enterprise annual overage invoice failures suspend or keep suspended annual entitlement until recovery, and paid recovery restores the correct active/expired state.
- [x] Enterprise ramp-up invoice failures suspend or keep suspended ramp-up entitlement until recovery, and paid recovery restores the correct active/expired state.
- [x] Owner Billing page and Application admin billing view show latest Enterprise invoice status, hosted invoice URL, and suspended/payment-gated state.
- [x] Billing reconciliation repairs clearly paid missed Enterprise invoices and records drift for ambiguous terminal states.
- [x] Backend, frontend, and Application admin tests cover annual upfront, annual overage, and ramp-up invoice paid/failure/recovery paths.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Complete Enterprise invoice lifecycle handling for live Workspace billing readiness.

**Current behavior:**
Workspace billing already has production-readiness foundations for Stripe webhook outcome storage, signature verification, explicit Stripe event routing, Billing reconciliation drift, Owner billing activity, and Application admin billing review. Enterprise ramp-up, Enterprise annual commitment, annual upfront invoices, and annual overage invoices are also represented in Workspace billing state and user/admin summaries.

The remaining gap is lifecycle completeness for Enterprise invoices. Paid Enterprise annual upfront invoices can activate annual entitlement, and paid recovery paths exist for some Enterprise ramp-up and annual overage invoices. However, customer-action, finalization-failed, payment-failed, voided, and uncollectible Enterprise invoice states are not consistently applied for every Enterprise invoice reference. Those states can be observed by Stripe without clearly updating the stored Enterprise invoice status, preserving hosted invoice actions, keeping annual upfront entitlement payment-gated, or suspending/restoring ramp-up and overage entitlement deterministically.

**Desired behavior:**
Enterprise invoice lifecycle handling should treat every supported Stripe invoice event as a first-class Workspace billing signal for the relevant Enterprise billing action.

Annual upfront invoices should keep the Enterprise annual commitment payment-gated until a paid invoice is observed. Customer-action, finalization-failed, payment-failed, voided, and uncollectible annual upfront invoice events should persist the latest invoice status and hosted invoice URL when available, but must not activate Enterprise annual entitlement. A paid annual upfront invoice should activate the annual commitment exactly once, preserve or update the hosted invoice URL, and remain idempotent across duplicate or replayed Stripe events.

Enterprise annual overage invoices should suspend or keep suspended Enterprise annual entitlement for unpaid negative states such as payment failed, action required, finalization failed, voided, or uncollectible. A later paid event for the same known overage invoice should restore the Enterprise annual commitment to active when the annual term is still current, or expired when the term has ended.

Enterprise ramp-up invoices should behave the same way for ramp-up terms: unpaid negative states suspend or keep suspended ramp-up entitlement, and paid recovery restores active or expired state according to the ramp-up term dates.

Owner Billing page and Application admin billing review should show the latest Enterprise invoice status, hosted invoice action when Stripe provides one, and payment-gated or suspended Enterprise state without exposing raw Stripe payloads, internal Stripe object IDs, secrets, payment method details, or unnecessary customer PII.

Billing reconciliation should repair clearly paid missed Enterprise invoices through the same idempotent activation/recovery paths used by webhooks. Ambiguous terminal states, metadata mismatches, or known invoice status conflicts that cannot be safely repaired should be recorded as Billing reconciliation drift for Application admin review rather than silently mutating entitlement.

**Key interfaces:**
- Stripe billing event router — should route paid, customer-action, finalization-failed, payment-failed, voided, and uncollectible invoice events to Enterprise annual upfront, Enterprise annual overage, and Enterprise ramp-up handlers when the invoice metadata identifies those billing actions.
- Enterprise invoice state in Workspace billing control data — should persist the latest invoice status and hosted invoice URL for annual upfront, annual overage, and ramp-up invoices.
- Enterprise entitlement summary — should continue deriving active, pending payment, suspended, and expired Enterprise state from Workspace billing control data without changing Enterprise pricing or product-limit semantics.
- Billing reconciliation — should distinguish clearly repairable paid Enterprise invoices from ambiguous or terminal unpaid drift.
- Owner billing activity and Application admin billing review responses — should surface Enterprise invoice state and hosted invoice action safely.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Enterprise annual upfront invoice events for payment action required, finalization failed, payment failed, voided, and marked uncollectible persist the latest invoice status and hosted invoice URL without activating Enterprise annual entitlement.

- [ ] Enterprise annual overage invoice negative states suspend or keep suspended Enterprise annual entitlement, persist invoice status/hosted URL, and never grant Credits or other prepaid entitlement.
- [ ] Enterprise annual overage paid recovery restores Enterprise annual entitlement to active when the annual term is current and expired when the annual term has ended.
