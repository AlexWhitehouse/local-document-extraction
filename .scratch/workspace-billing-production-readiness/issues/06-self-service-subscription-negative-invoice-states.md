# Self-Service Subscription Negative Invoice States

Category: enhancement
Status: completed

## What to build

Complete owner-visible and admin-visible handling for self-service subscription invoices that are not paid. Payment action required, finalization failed, payment failed, voided, marked uncollectible, and later recovery should update Workspace billing state without granting unpaid access or Credits.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Subscription invoice payment action required is persisted with hosted invoice/payment URL when Stripe provides one and does not grant entitlement or Credits.
- [x] Subscription invoice finalization failed is persisted with actionable owner/admin state, including tax/location failure signals when present, and does not grant entitlement or Credits.
- [x] Subscription invoice payment failed keeps or moves the Workspace to the correct unpaid billing state for the relevant period.
- [x] Subscription invoice voided and marked uncollectible are handled as terminal unpaid states or recorded as drift when they cannot be safely applied.
- [x] A later paid invoice or payment recovery restores paid entitlement and Included Credits exactly once through the paid invoice path.
- [x] Owner Billing page and Application admin billing state expose the new invoice states without leaking sensitive Stripe data.
- [x] Backend, frontend Billing page, and Application admin tests cover each negative/terminal state and recovery.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Complete non-granting self-service subscription invoice states for live Stripe billing.

**Current behavior:**
Workspace billing already has the production webhook outcome store, signature verification, explicit routing, and admin diagnostic foundations needed for this issue. Paid self-service subscription invoices activate paid entitlement and grant Included Credits through the paid invoice path. Failed self-service subscription invoices can move a Workspace into Unpaid billing state, and owner billing surfaces can show an unpaid subscription invoice with a hosted invoice action.

The remaining gap is that not every relevant negative or terminal self-service subscription invoice event is treated as a first-class Workspace billing outcome. Subscription invoices that require customer action, fail finalization, become void, or become marked uncollectible may be ignored or handled only indirectly through reconciliation. That can leave owner/admin surfaces without actionable payment state, and live billing could retain confusing or stale self-service subscription state.

**Desired behavior:**
All supported self-service subscription invoice outcomes should update Workspace billing state without granting unpaid access or Credits.

When a subscription invoice requires payment action, the invoice state should be persisted with any Stripe-hosted invoice or payment URL available to the app. The Workspace should remain or become unpaid for the relevant Billing period, and no Included Credits should be granted until the paid invoice path observes a successful payment.

When subscription invoice finalization fails, the app should persist an actionable owner/admin state. If Stripe provides tax, automatic tax, billing address, customer location, or similar finalization-failure signals consumed by the app, preserve a non-secret normalized summary that explains what kind of customer/operator action is needed. Do not store raw Stripe payloads, payment method details, secrets, or unnecessary customer PII.

When subscription invoice payment fails, the Workspace should remain or become Unpaid billing state for the invoice period, using the invoice period and subscription metadata to avoid carrying paid entitlement into an unpaid period.

When subscription invoices are voided or marked uncollectible, the system should either apply a deterministic terminal unpaid state for the known Workspace billing invoice or record Billing reconciliation drift when the event cannot be safely applied.

A later paid invoice or payment recovery should restore paid entitlement and grant Included Credits exactly once through the existing paid invoice behavior. Duplicate, delayed, out-of-order, and replayed events must not duplicate Included Credits, paid entitlement changes, owner billing activity, or admin diagnostics.

Owner-facing billing state and the Application admin billing view should expose these negative and terminal invoice states with clear, non-secret status and hosted payment actions when available.

**Key interfaces:**
- Stripe billing webhook event routing — should explicitly support self-service subscription invoice events for payment action required, finalization failed, payment failed, voided, marked uncollectible, and later paid recovery.
- Self-service subscription invoice state in Workspace billing control data — should preserve invoice ID, invoice status, hosted invoice/payment URL, relevant Billing period, and non-secret finalization-failure diagnostics where applicable.
- Active entitlement resolution — should ensure unpaid, action-required, finalization-failed, voided, or uncollectible self-service subscription invoice states do not grant paid entitlement or Included Credits.
- Billing ledger Included Credit grants — should remain idempotent and only grant through paid invoice processing.
- Owner billing summary/activity — should expose subscription invoice status and hosted payment action without leaking internal Stripe IDs beyond safe customer-facing references.
- Application admin billing state — should expose negative/terminal subscription invoice states and any reconciliation drift or failed-event diagnostics needed for support review.
- Billing reconciliation — should detect missed terminal invoice states and payment recovery, repairing only safe paid outcomes through idempotent paid invoice paths and recording drift for ambiguous cases.
