# Paid Plan Downgrade Invoice Activation

Category: enhancement
Status: completed

## What to build

Make a paid Stripe Plan downgrade invoice activate the lower paid Workspace plan for the new Billing period. The flow should behave like other paid subscription invoices: it should update Stripe subscription references, grant Included Credits exactly once for the new lower plan period, preserve Purchased Credits, and leave scheduled entitlement state deterministic.

- [x] Subscription invoice classification recognizes Plan downgrade invoices as a typed paid subscription billing action.
- [x] A paid downgrade invoice activates the lower paid plan for the invoice Billing period and updates Stripe customer, subscription, subscription item, invoice, and hosted invoice references.
- [x] Included Credits for the lower paid plan are granted exactly once for the new period, including duplicate webhook delivery and manual replay.
- [x] Purchased Credits remain unchanged across the downgrade activation.
- [x] Scheduled entitlement state is cleared, updated, or retained according to a documented deterministic rule.
- [x] Owner billing summary/activity and Application admin billing state show the lower paid entitlement and related invoice state.
- [x] Backend tests cover paid downgrade, duplicate delivery, purchased-credit preservation, included-credit idempotency, and reconciliation repair for a missed paid downgrade invoice.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Treat paid Plan downgrade invoices as first-class paid subscription invoices that activate the lower paid Workspace plan for the new Billing period.

**Current behavior:**
Workspace owners can schedule a paid Plan downgrade, such as Max to Pro, for the next Billing period. The Stripe subscription update carries `subscription_downgrade` metadata with the target plan, and the owner-facing summary can show the scheduled lower entitlement while the current paid period remains active.

Paid subscription invoice handling and Billing reconciliation already support subscription starts, Plan upgrades, and renewals. They activate the paid entitlement, store Stripe customer/subscription/subscription item/invoice references, and grant Included Credits through the Billing ledger idempotently. A paid invoice whose billing action is `subscription_downgrade` is not yet treated as one of those supported subscription invoice actions, so the paid lower-plan invoice can be ignored rather than activating the lower paid plan for the new period.

**Desired behavior:**
A paid Stripe invoice for a scheduled Plan downgrade should be classified as a typed paid subscription billing action. When Stripe reports the invoice as paid, Workspace billing should activate the target lower paid plan for the invoice Billing period, update Stripe customer, subscription, subscription item, invoice status, and hosted invoice URL references, and grant exactly that lower plan's Included Credits for the new period.

The downgrade activation must not remove or rewrite Purchased Credits. Duplicate webhook delivery, duplicate business-object delivery, manual replay, and scheduled Billing reconciliation repair should all converge without duplicating Included Credits or owner/admin activity. Scheduled entitlement control state must follow one documented deterministic rule after activation, such as clearing the scheduled entitlement once the invoice period has become the active lower paid period.

Owner billing summary/activity and Application admin billing state should show the lower paid entitlement and related paid invoice state after activation. The behavior should reuse the same subscription invoice contract as starts, upgrades, and renewals rather than adding a separate ad hoc downgrade path.

**Key interfaces:**
- Stripe subscription invoice action classification — should recognize `subscription_downgrade` as a supported typed paid subscription billing action alongside start, upgrade, and renewal actions.
- Paid subscription invoice application — should resolve Workspace, target plan, Billing period, Stripe customer, subscription, subscription item, invoice ID, invoice status, and hosted invoice URL from the paid Stripe invoice payload.
- Workspace billing control data — should persist the lower paid plan as active for the invoice Billing period and apply a deterministic scheduled entitlement cleanup/update rule.
- Billing ledger Included Credit grant — should grant the lower plan's Included Credits with Stripe-invoice idempotency and must not mutate Purchased Credit ledger entries.
- Billing reconciliation — should repair missed paid Plan downgrade invoices through the same idempotent activation and Included Credit grant behavior as webhook processing.
- Owner billing summary/activity and Application admin billing state — should expose the active lower paid entitlement and related invoice state without showing raw Stripe payloads or secrets.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Subscription invoice classification recognizes `subscription_downgrade` as a typed paid subscription billing action.
- [ ] A paid downgrade invoice activates the lower paid plan for the invoice Billing period.
- [ ] Stripe customer, subscription, subscription item, invoice status, and hosted invoice URL references are updated from the paid downgrade invoice.
- [ ] Included Credits for the lower paid plan are granted exactly once for the new period.
- [ ] Duplicate webhook delivery, duplicate business-object delivery, manual replay, and scheduled Billing reconciliation repair do not duplicate Included Credits or owner/admin activity.
- [ ] Purchased Credits remain unchanged across downgrade activation.
- [ ] Scheduled entitlement state is cleared, updated, or retained according to a documented deterministic rule.
- [ ] Owner billing summary/activity shows the lower paid entitlement and related paid invoice state.
- [ ] Application admin billing state shows the lower paid entitlement and related invoice state.
- [ ] Backend coverage includes paid downgrade activation, duplicate delivery, Purchased Credit preservation, Included Credit idempotency, and reconciliation repair for a missed paid downgrade invoice.

**Out of scope:**
- Changing Free, Pro, Max, Enterprise, Credit pack, or Workspace billing authority product semantics.
- Changing plan prices, Credit pack sizes, coupons, trials, refunds, disputes, chargebacks, tax registration, or Stripe Customer Portal behavior.
- Reworking the Billing ledger authority model or rewriting historical ledger entries.
- Adding new Stripe lifecycle event support beyond what is needed for paid Plan downgrade invoice activation and reconciliation repair.
- Entering, printing, committing, or documenting Stripe secrets, webhook secrets, raw Stripe payloads, or production credentials.
