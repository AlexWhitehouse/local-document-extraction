## What to build

Extend Workspace deletion so queued **Extraction jobs**, delayed queue deliveries, and retry scheduling cannot reopen or recreate authoritative Workspace product data after deletion. The Local extraction runner must distinguish a deleted or deleting Workspace from a valid Workspace with no claimable job and must stop before creating a product store or scheduling another attempt.

This slice is complete when a real Workspace can be deleted, an already-enqueued job can be delivered afterward, and the Workspace product database remains absent without a new lifecycle event or retry.

- [ ] Runner recovery does not initialize a missing product store for a Workspace whose access has been revoked.
- [ ] Retryable processing failures do not schedule a subsequent attempt once Workspace deletion has begun.
- [ ] Late queued work produces no completed or failed Extraction job lifecycle write for the deleted Workspace.
- [ ] Repeated late queue deliveries remain harmless and do not emit Workspace live updates or product analytics for nonexistent lifecycle changes.
- [ ] Existing queued, retry, stale-processing, and terminal-attempt behavior for active Workspaces remains unchanged.
- [ ] Tests reproduce the previously observed late-runner database recreation and prove that it no longer occurs through the public runner and deletion Interfaces.
- [ ] Focused runner, Workspace deletion, and backend typecheck checks pass.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Ensure late queue deliveries and recovery cannot recreate hard-erased Workspace product data.

**Current behavior:**
The Local extraction runner opens product storage through the initializer for every queue delivery. A job delivered after Workspace deletion therefore creates a new empty database, and the runner cannot tell that the missing data represents a deleted Workspace rather than an active Workspace with no claimable job.

**Desired behavior:**
Runner work must first confirm that the Workspace still exists in Workspace control data and then open only existing product data. A deleted Workspace, a missing product store, or a Workspace that becomes unavailable before retry scheduling is a harmless no-op: it produces no lifecycle mutation, retry delivery, live update, or analytics event.

**Key interfaces:**
- `LocalWorkspaceControl` — provides a non-repairing existence check suitable for background work.
- `Local extraction runner` — receives Workspace availability and a non-creating product-store opener; its queue-delivery and recovery paths use both before persisted lifecycle work.
- Workspace product-store lifecycle — separates opening an existing store from explicit initialization so background work cannot create product data.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A queued job delivered after hard deletion leaves the Workspace product database absent, even for repeated delivery.
- [ ] Recovery skips Workspace IDs whose control access no longer exists and never creates a missing product store.
- [ ] A retryable failure does not requeue or schedule another attempt after Workspace availability has ended.
- [ ] Deleted-Workspace late work emits no lifecycle writes, live updates, or product analytics; active Workspace queue, retry, stale-processing, and terminal behavior remains unchanged.
- [ ] Focused runner/deletion tests and backend typecheck pass.

**Out of scope:**
- Coordinating simultaneous product HTTP requests or active claims with deletion, cancellation/draining of in-flight extraction, durable deletion intent and restart recovery, and browser invalidation.
- Changes to document deletion, profile, membership, invitation, or job-listing capabilities.

## Completion

Implemented non-creating product-store access for background work, control-data Workspace availability checks, and deletion-aware recovery/retry guards. Full backend/frontend tests, lint, backend typecheck, and frontend production build passed.
