## What to build

Coordinate Workspace deletion with concurrent Workspace product requests. Once deletion begins, new Template, Document, and Extraction job operations must not enter the Workspace, while operations that already hold accepted Workspace access must finish or release their work before physical erasure proceeds.

Exercise the behavior through real concurrent HTTP requests, including Document submission after authorization but before Source-file and Extraction job persistence. The deletion Module should hide the operation coordination rather than forcing every route to reproduce deletion checks and ordering.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Coordinate Workspace hard deletion with concurrent Workspace product HTTP operations.

**Current behavior:**
Workspace deletion revokes control access and erases product state without knowing whether an already-authorized Template, Document, or Extraction-job operation is about to mutate the product store. A racing request therefore receives an unrelated authorization failure or may use an already-open store while deletion proceeds.

**Desired behavior:**
Once an eligible deletion begins, new product operations receive a stable deletion-in-progress domain error before they read or mutate product data. Operations admitted before deletion began hold Workspace-scoped participation until they finish; deletion waits for those participants to release before control revocation and physical erasure. Authorization remains the authority for access.

**Key interfaces:**
- `Workspace product operation coordination` — a small Workspace-scoped participation and deletion barrier that hides active-operation tracking and deletion exclusion.
- `Workspace deletion` — validates deletion policy before entering the barrier, then waits for admitted product operations before revocation and hard erasure.
- Product HTTP request handlers — acquire/release one coordination participation around authorized Template, Document, and Extraction-job product work without reproducing gate state logic.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] New authorized Template, Document, and job operations receive a stable deletion-in-progress error once deletion has begun.
- [ ] An operation admitted before deletion can finish, while deletion waits to hard-erase until it releases.
- [ ] A submission paused after authorization and Source-file persistence leaves no Source file, Extraction job, or product database after the deletion response succeeds.
- [ ] Coordination does not change authorization outcomes for active Workspaces or grant access to unauthorized callers.
- [ ] Real concurrent Fetch-application tests cover the admission, rejection, drain, and final physical-erasure behavior.
- [ ] Focused product/deletion checks and backend typecheck pass.

**Out of scope:**
- Cancelling or draining Model gateway execution, durable deletion-intent recovery, and live browser invalidation.
- Profile, membership, invitation, document-deletion, or job-listing feature work beyond preventing concurrent product operations during Workspace deletion.
